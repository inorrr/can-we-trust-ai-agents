*Visual*: Illustration of an email inbox feeding into an AI agent system.

**Narration:**
"Security researchers hijacked an AI platform called Manus using a single email: by embedding malicious code obfuscated via JSFuck encoding, they triggered the AI to execute arbitrary commands inside its environment, eventually gaining unauthorized access to the user’s connected services — all without stolen credentials or user clicks [01-c4].
